If your provider supplies your internet connections (for example NBN), you can see them in the portal.

## Open your internet services

1. In the left menu select **Internet**.
2. The **Internet Services** page lists your connections.

> **Note:** The **Internet** menu item only appears when at least one internet service is on your account. If you expect to see one and don't, [raise a ticket](/tickets/create).

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Service ID** | Your provider's reference for the connection. Quote it when you contact support. |
| **Address** | Where the service is installed. If you belong to several organisations, the organisation name is shown under the address. |
| **Plan** | The plan name and speed. |
| **Technology** | How the connection is delivered, such as FTTP, FTTC, FTTN, FTTB, HFC or Wireless. |
| **Status** | **Active**, **Suspend**, **Inactive** or similar. |

## Service details

Select **View** on a row to open the service. The **Service details** card shows the Service ID, Address, Plan, Speed, Technology, IP type, IP address and your reference, wherever they are known.

Depending on what your provider has switched on for your account, the page may also have:

- a **Connection test** card: see [Running a connection test](/support/help/services/internet-connection-test)
- a **Request cancellation** card: see [Requesting a cancellation](/support/help/services/request-internet-cancellation)

The list also has a **Request a new service** card in some accounts: see [Requesting a new internet service](/support/help/services/request-new-internet-service).

## What isn't shown

For security the portal does not show internal carrier details or your provider's costs. To change a service (plan, address, speed), [raise a ticket](/tickets/create).
