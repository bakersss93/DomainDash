You can't cancel a connection yourself. When your provider has enabled cancellation requests, you can log one for your own services. Your service stays active until your provider confirms the cancellation with you.

## Is it available to me?

The **Request cancellation** card appears on a service's page only when:

- your provider has enabled cancellation requests for customers, and
- your account has the `internet.cancel` permission.

You can only request cancellation of services belonging to your own organisation.

## Make a request

1. In the left menu select **Internet**, then **View** next to the service.
2. In the **Request cancellation** card, optionally write a **Reason**.
3. Select **Request cancellation** and confirm "Log a cancellation request for this service?".

You see "Your cancellation request has been logged with our support team. The service stays active until we confirm."

> **Warning:** Cancelling a connection can mean losing your phone line or other services that depend on it, and may involve fees or notice periods under your agreement. Your provider will go through this with you before anything is cancelled.

## Changed your mind?

[Raise a ticket](/tickets/create) straight away and mention the Service ID. Until the cancellation is confirmed, nothing has changed.
