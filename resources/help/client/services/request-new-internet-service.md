You can't order a connection yourself. When your provider has enabled requests, the form below logs a request with their support team, and nothing is ordered until they confirm with you.

## Is it available to me?

The **Request a new service** card appears on the **Internet Services** page only when:

- your provider has enabled new-service requests for customers, and
- your account has the `internet.order` permission.

If you don't see it, [raise a ticket](/tickets/create) with the same details instead.

## Make a request

1. In the left menu select **Internet**.
2. Scroll to **Request a new service**.
3. If you belong to more than one organisation, choose the **Account**.
4. Enter the **Service address**.
5. Optionally add a **Preferred speed** and any **Notes** (for example, the best contact time or whether it's a move from another provider).
6. Select **Request new service**.

You see "Your request has been logged with our support team." and your provider's team follows it up. It may also appear under **Support** > **View Support Requests**, depending on how your provider has set up ticket types.

> **Note:** Requests are checked against your provider's support system. If it isn't set up for your organisation yet you'll see a message asking you to contact them directly.

## What happens next

Your provider checks whether the address can be connected and contacts you with options and pricing. Nothing changes on your account until you agree.
