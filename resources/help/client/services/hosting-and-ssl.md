Your hosting services and SSL certificates are shown, read-only, on your **Dashboard**. Only services belonging to your organisation appear.

## See your services

1. Select **Dashboard** in the left menu.
2. Look at the summary tiles at the top: **Domains**, **Hosting services**, **SSL certificates** and **Internet services**, each with a count. The Domains and SSL tiles also say how many are expiring soon.
3. Use the **Search services, status, or client** box and **Search** to filter everything on the page.

### Hosting Services

The **Hosting Services** table lists, for each service:

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Domain** | The main domain of the hosting service. |
| **Plan** | Your hosting plan. |
| **Username** | The hosting account username. |
| **Client** | Which of your organisations it belongs to. |
| **Renewal** | The next renewal date. |

### SSL Certificates

The **SSL Certificates** table lists the **Common Name** (the website name it protects), **Product**, **Client**, **Expires** date and **Status**. A row is highlighted in red when the certificate expires in 30 days or less.

> **Warning:** An expired SSL certificate makes browsers show a security warning on your website. If a certificate is highlighted, [raise a ticket](/tickets/create) and choose **SSL** as the service category so we can renew it.

## What you can't do here

Changing, renewing or purchasing hosting and SSL is done by your provider's staff. To ask for a change, [raise a ticket](/tickets/create) and pick **Web Hosting** or **SSL** as the **Service Category**, then choose the specific service under **Related Service**.

The dashboard shows up to 50 of each kind. Use the search box to narrow the list if you have many.
