## Open a ticket

1. Open **Support** > **View Support Requests**.
2. Select the ticket. The page is titled **Ticket #** followed by its number, with the summary underneath.

At the top you see the **Client**, **Status**, **Service**, **Type** and **Last Update**. Below is **Client Visible Communications / Email Actions**: the messages and updates between you and the support team. Internal notes are not shown.

## Send a reply

1. Select **Submit Reply**.
2. Type your message in the **Reply to technician / provide update** box.
3. Select **Submit Reply** in the window.

You return to the ticket with "Reply sent to Halo ticket."

> **Tip:** Answer the technician's question directly and keep the reply on one topic. If you have a different problem, log a new ticket.

## Close a ticket

1. Select **Close Ticket**.
2. Enter a **Reason for closure**, such as "Fixed after restarting the router".
3. Select **Confirm Close Ticket**.

> **Warning:** Close a ticket only when you're happy it's resolved. If the problem comes back, log a new ticket and mention the old ticket number.

## Open the ticket in the support portal

If your provider's support portal link is available, an **Open in Client Portal** button opens the same ticket there.

## Can't find your ticket?

- Check you've selected the right **Client** and that the **Filter by Service** and **Filter by Type** boxes are on their "All" settings.
- Use **Load Next 25** if the ticket is older.
- Tickets of types your provider hasn't set up for the portal aren't listed. [Raise a ticket](/tickets/create) or contact your provider.
