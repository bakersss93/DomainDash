Use a ticket for anything you can't fix from the guides: a problem, a question, or a request to change one of your services.

## Log a ticket

1. In the left menu open **Support**, then select **Log Support Ticket**.
2. Fill in the form:
   - **Subject**: a short summary, such as "Email not arriving for contoso.com".
   - **Message**: what happened, what you expected, and when it started. Include any error text and what you've already tried.
   - **Client**: choose your organisation if you belong to more than one.
   - **Ticket Type**: **Support/Issue** for something that's broken or confusing, or **Service Request** to ask for a change.
   - **Service Category**: **Domain**, **Web Hosting** or **SSL**.
   - **Related Service**: pick the specific domain, hosting service or certificate the ticket is about. The list changes to match the category and client you chose.
3. Select **Submit Ticket**. You see "Ticket logged to HaloPSA".

> **Tip:** One ticket per problem. Put the domain name or Service ID in the message so we can find it quickly.

> **Note:** If you see "The selected client is not linked to HaloPSA" or a message that ticketing isn't configured, your provider needs to finish setting up your account. Contact them directly.

## Find your tickets

1. Open **Support** and select **View Support Requests**.
2. Choose the **Client**, and optionally **Filter by Service** and **Filter by Type**, then select **Apply**. **Clear** resets the filters.
3. The table shows **ID**, **Summary**, **Service**, **Type**, **Status** and **Updated**. Select **Load Next 25** to see older tickets.
4. Select a ticket to open it.

You only see tickets of the types your provider has set up for the portal, and only for your own organisation.

## Reply to or close a ticket

See [Replying to and closing tickets](/support/help/support/replying-to-tickets).

## Urgent problems

If your internet or email is down for the whole business, don't wait for a reply: phone your provider using the number on your agreement.
