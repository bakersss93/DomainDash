## The notification bell

The bell icon at the top right of every page shows notifications for your account. A small badge with a number means you have notifications to read.

1. Select the bell. The **Notifications** panel opens.
2. Read the items. Each has a title, some text and, where relevant, a link to the page it is about.
3. Select **Mark as read** on an item, or **Mark all read** at the top of the panel to clear the list.
4. Select the **X** to close the panel.

Notifications are personal to you. Other people on your account don't see yours.

> **Note:** System alerts, running background jobs and similar items in the panel are for your provider's administrators, not for customer accounts.

## Email reminders

Your provider can also send reminder emails, for example before a domain or an SSL certificate is due to expire. These come from your provider's configured email address. If you think reminders are missing:

- Check your spam folder.
- Make sure the **Email** in **Account Settings** is correct. See [Updating your account details](/support/help/getting-started/update-your-account).
- [Raise a ticket](/tickets/create) and ask which reminders are set up for you.

## Expiry highlights on the dashboard

Domains and SSL certificates that expire within 30 days are highlighted in red on the **Dashboard**, and the summary tiles count how many are expiring soon. See [Viewing your hosting and SSL certificates](/support/help/services/hosting-and-ssl).
