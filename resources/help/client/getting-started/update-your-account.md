You can change your own name, email address and password at any time from **Account Settings**.

## Open Account Settings

1. Select your name at the top right of any page.
2. Select **Account Settings**.

If your account is linked to Microsoft sign-in, a line in this window shows the linked Microsoft account.

## Change your name or email address

1. In **Account Settings**, edit **Name** or **Email**.
2. Select **Save Profile**.

> **Warning:** Changing your **Email** needs your current password, and your other signed-in sessions are ended. Your email is also where password-reset links are sent, so use an address you can read.

## Change your password

1. In **Account Settings**, select **Change Password**.
2. Enter your **Current password**, then your **New password** and **Confirm new password**.
3. If MFA is set up on your account, enter the current code in **MFA code required for password change**.
4. Select **Update Password**.

Your other sessions are signed out when the password changes. In production a new password must be at least 12 characters with upper and lower case letters and a number, and must not appear in known breaches.

> **Tip:** Use a password manager to create and store a long, unique password.

## Re-enrol MFA

**Re-enroll MFA** in the same window moves MFA to a new phone. See [Setting up and using MFA](/support/help/getting-started/setting-up-mfa).

## Can't change your password?

If your organisation uses **Microsoft only** sign-in, your portal password is not used to sign in, and you change your Microsoft password through Microsoft instead. For anything else, [raise a ticket](/tickets/create).
